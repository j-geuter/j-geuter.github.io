Two new follow-up works to boomerang distillation out: [Understanding Layer Patching in Model Size Interpolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08170), accepted to the AdaptFM workshop at ICML 2026, and [Thinking at the Right Size: Amortized Distillation Across Post-Trained LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22854), accepted to EMNLP 2026! These papers explore extending [boomerang distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05064) to post-trained models, and better understanding its layer patching mechanism.
