I helped train [IFM's](https://ifm.ai/) suite of fully [open-source language models](https://ifm.ai/k2/), coming in sizes from 0.9B to 375B! We also developed Uno, a lossless discrete diffusion framework which accelerates decoding by up to 2.5x compared to autoregressive sampling. We provide Uno adapters for Qwen3-8B and our own 0.9B and 7B models. Check out the paper: [Unlocking Lossless Speedups in LLMs via Discrete Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04010).
