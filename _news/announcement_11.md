We release Jeff, a suite of state-of-the-art decision models that outperform [TypeSafe's](https://typesafe.ai/) Jev model across a wide range of tasks! We open-source our [models](https://huggingface.co/collections/jgeuter/jeff-10), [serving code](https://github.com/j-geuter/Jeff), and [OpenDecisions](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jgeuter/OpenDecisions), a novel decision model validation dataset. Check out the tech report: [Releasing the Inner Jev: A Simple Recipe for Open Decision Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17205).
