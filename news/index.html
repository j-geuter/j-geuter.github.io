Oct 24, 2026 We release Jeff, a suite of state-of-the-art decision models that outperform TypeSafe’s Jev model across a wide range of tasks! We open-source our models, serving code, and OpenDecisions, a novel decision model validation dataset. Check out the tech report: Releasing the Inner Jev: A Simple Recipe for Open Decision Models.
Sep 24, 2026 Our paper Understanding Layer Patching in Model Size Interpolation was accepted to NeurIPS 2026!
Sep 04, 2026 I helped train IFM’s suite of fully open-source language models, coming in sizes from 0.9B to 375B! We also developed Uno, a lossless discrete diffusion framework which accelerates decoding by up to 2.5x compared to autoregressive sampling. We provide Uno adapters for Qwen3-8B and our own 0.9B and 7B models. Check out the paper Unlocking Lossless Speedups in LLMs via Discrete Diffusion and the blog post.
Aug 26, 2026 Two new follow-up works to boomerang distillation out: Understanding Layer Patching in Model Size Interpolation, accepted to the AdaptFM workshop at ICML 2026, and Thinking at the Right Size: Amortized Distillation Across Post-Trained LLMs, accepted to EMNLP 2026! These papers explore extending boomerang distillation to post-trained models, and better understanding its layer patching mechanism.
